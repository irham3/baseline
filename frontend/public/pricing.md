# Pricing — Baseline

Baseline is an AI pre-deal decision system for Indonesian freelancers: brief critique and clarification for any profession, plus transparent price floor calculation on calibrated work templates (short-form video today).

## Free Tier (Guest & Community)
- Price: $0 / IDR 0
- Account: No sign-up required
- Includes:
  - Full client brief scope analysis and hidden work detection
  - Interactive Judge Mode simulation
  - Instant price floor calculation based on custom take-home pay and overhead
  - 3-option deal proposal copy generator
  - Shareable pre-deal agreement links

## Pro / Freelancer Account
- Price: $0 (Current Open Release)
- Account: Google Sign-In or Email / Password
- Includes:
  - Persistent cost profile storage (target take-home, overhead, reserve buffer, billable utilization)
  - Historical project calibration tracking (estimated vs actual hours)
  - Multi-project median correction factors
  - Saved brief analyses and agreement history
