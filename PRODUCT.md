# Baseline Product Truth

## Platform

Web application built for Emergent publishing.

## Users

Freelance short-form video editors and small creative studios in Indonesia who receive ambiguous Reels or TikTok briefs through WhatsApp, Instagram, referrals, or communities.

## Product Purpose

Baseline helps a freelancer decide what to quote before saying yes. It turns a messy client brief into evidence-backed scope, hidden variables, clarification questions, an hour range, a transparent price floor, deal options, WhatsApp copy, and a public Agreement Sheet.

## Positioning

Baseline is a pre-deal scope check for short-form video freelancers. It is not a marketplace, CRM, legal contract generator, payment product, autonomous negotiation agent, or market-rate oracle.

## Operating Context

The contest P0 must work without login through a deterministic seeded sample and Judge Mode. Login is only for saving a freelancer's cost profile, analyses, and one-project Personal Estimation Memory. Client-facing agreement links must never require login.

## Capabilities And Constraints

- AI may extract scope evidence and write editable copy.
- AI must not calculate prices, margins, hours, or risk scores.
- Pricing comes from deterministic code and visible assumptions.
- Public agreement pages must not expose raw briefs, internal costs, margins, break-even, owner data, or private calibration history.
- Demo values are illustrative and must be labeled as demo data.
- P0 focuses on short-form video only.

## Brand Commitments

- Public UI, generated labels, and client-facing copy should be direct, professional English.
- The system may understand informal Indonesian briefs, but visible product language must stay consistently English.
- WhatsApp drafts and Agreement Sheets should sound like professional client communication.
- Motion should show progress, reveal evidence, confirm selection, or clarify state changes.

## Evidence On Hand

The product plan cites Indonesian creative-work informality and broader freelance scope-creep signals. These support the problem category, but they do not prove guaranteed profit, market pricing, or realized savings. Any impact claim must stay projected unless backed by pilot evidence.

## Product Principles

- Show the hidden work before the freelancer quotes.
- Prefer ranges and named assumptions over false precision.
- Make every CTA lead to a working product step.
- Keep the P0 flow narrow, fast, mobile-friendly, and resilient when live AI fails.

## Accessibility And Inclusion

The UI should work at 375px width, provide visible focus states, avoid clipped text, respect reduced motion, keep touch targets comfortable, and never depend on color alone for important status.
